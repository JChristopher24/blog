# Vacation

### My Summer was mostly split into three parts, Vacation, Work and School. I spent my vacation in Jamaica, which I consider a place where I could be by myself whereas when I'm in NYC I always feel like my mind can be crowded with all the things I have to do wether it's in school or decisions I'd have to make for college or my future career. This vacation really gave me a chance to relax and enjoy being a kid without havng to think about school.

# Internship at GrowHouse

### My summer internship really interested me, from creating a land use plan to reaching out to city officials there were many things we had to do but, researching zoning and land use planning showed me how neighborhood projects are brought into existence and turned into buildings and can support the communtiy they are built in. At first it was awkward to interact with new people I didn't know but I quickly became comfortable working with them.
